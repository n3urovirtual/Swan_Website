Where SWAN works

Seven partners, seven countries

Pilot interventions will run in Spain, Greece and Bulgaria. Research and coordination are shared across the whole consortium.

The SWAN Consortium consists of 7 partners from 7 countries: Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Slovenia, Germany, France and Spain. Select a partner to read more about them.

The consortium

Who does what

Coordinator

International Table Tennis Federation Foundation

Germany

The humanitarian arm of the International Table Tennis Federation. ITTF Foundation harnesses the inclusive and health-enhancing qualities of table tennis to drive social development and foster peace worldwide, through programmes such as Table Tennis for All (TT4ALL) and Table Tennis for Health (TT4HEALTH). In SWAN it coordinates the consortium and leads the Best Practices Report.

Visit website
Beneficiary

University of Ljubljana — Faculty of Sport

Slovenia

Slovenia’s foremost institution for education, research and professional development in kinesiology and sport. Its research spans the bio-psycho-social aspects of kinesiology as well as high-performance training. In SWAN it leads Deliverable 2.1 Research Insights and the Literature Review.

Visit website
Beneficiary

KTG Research & Innovation

Cyprus

A research and innovation organisation working across European projects. In SWAN it leads the Needs Assessment Report, mapping what practitioners and care settings across Europe need in order to run table tennis programmes for people with NDDs.

Visit website
Beneficiary

Panhellenic Athletic Club for Physically Disabled People

Greece

Founded in 1987 and based at the National Rehabilitation Center in Athens, PASKA was the first purely athletic club in Greece for people with mobility impairments and helped establish the Panhellenic Championship for people with disabilities. It hosts over 50 competitive athletes and runs mass-participation programmes promoting physical fitness, autonomy and social integration.

Visit website
Beneficiary

Foundation Compassion Alzheimer Bulgaria

Bulgaria

A Bulgarian foundation supporting people living with Alzheimer’s disease and the families and carers around them. In SWAN it brings care expertise to the adapted curriculum and hosts one of the pilot interventions.

Visit website
Beneficiary

French Table Tennis Federation

France

The national governing body for table tennis in France, responsible for the sport’s clubs, competitions and coach education across the country. In SWAN it contributes federation-scale expertise in taking table tennis programmes to clubs.

Visit website
Beneficiary

Alzheimer León

Spain

A Spanish association in León supporting people with Alzheimer’s disease and their families through therapy, day care and family support. In SWAN it brings that experience to the adapted curriculum and hosts one of the pilot interventions.

Visit website

Work with us

Interested in running adapted table tennis where you are?

SWAN is building a network of stakeholders in the therapeutic use of table tennis. Clubs, care homes, federations and patient associations are all welcome to get in touch.
