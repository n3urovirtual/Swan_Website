Partners

Seven organisations, one consortium

SWAN brings together researchers, sport federations, care organisations and technology partners from across the European Union — each contributing a different part of what it takes to put adapted table tennis into practice.

Where SWAN works

  • Slovenia
  • Greece
  • Bulgaria
  • Spain

Pilot interventions run in Greece, Bulgaria and Spain. Research and coordination are shared across the whole consortium.
